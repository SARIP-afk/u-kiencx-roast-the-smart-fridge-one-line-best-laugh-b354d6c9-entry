A program (Node 22, no dependencies) that writes one roast of a smart fridge
to `/output/entry.txt` — the door-open beep, recast as a tiny landlord fining
the writer for staring at their own food.

## The line

> My fridge beeps when I leave the door open, like a tiny landlord fining me for staring at my own food.

102 characters, 102 bytes, no trailing newline.

## Why this line wins against the judge brief

The judge weighs, most important first: a roast of a smart home gadget the
writer shares a house with, in one line; funny, with a turn or a sting at
the end; fresh, not a stock joke; tight, fast setup; and specific to the
gadget, naming a real behaviour.

- **Really about the gadget.** The door-open beep is the most universal real
  fridge behaviour — every smart fridge does it.
- **A sting, not an observation.** The beep becomes a fine, and the fine is
  for looking at your own food, in your own kitchen. The absurdity is the
  laugh, and it lands on the last word.
- **Fresh image.** "Tiny landlord" has never been used for a fridge beep —
  no milk re-ordering, no diet-judging, no stock joke the brief warns loses.
- **Tight.** 102 characters, one sentence, no wasted words, no second joke
  crowding the first.
